# Taxonomy Audit Notes — June 22, 2026

## Method

- Reviewed the existing descriptions and legacy taxonomy for all 102 activities.
- Preserved coherent classifications and feature tags, removed Sports, and added Dog-Friendly to all Outdoors and Water activities per Taylor's stated rule.
- Checked hybrid venues against current primary sources where the description alone did not settle the primary Type.

## Verified edge cases

- **Austin Nature & Science Center → Outdoors:** The City describes a site with forest trail, Dino Pit fossil dig, wildlife exhibits, pond trails, and indoor exhibits. The outdoor experience is sufficiently central to classify it as Outdoors; it is Kid-Friendly and explicitly not Dog-Friendly. [City of Austin](https://www.austintexas.gov/parks/plan-your-visit-austin-nature-science-center)
- **UMLAUF Sculpture Garden → Outdoors:** The venue identifies its sculpture garden, outdoor bronze sculptures, and outdoor programming as core to the visit. It remains Kid-Friendly but is not Dog-Friendly because its visitor policy excludes dogs other than service animals. [UMLAUF visitor information](https://www.umlaufsculpture.org/visit)
- **The Contemporary Austin at Laguna Gloria → Outdoors:** Its current visitor map centers the outdoor sculpture park, paths, grounds, and outdoor sculpture installations. [Laguna Gloria map](https://thecontemporaryaustin.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/The-Contemporary_Laguna-Gloria-Map_2026.pdf)
- **Chicken Shit Bingo → Indoors:** Its own description places the experience inside Ginny's Little Longhorn Saloon. The legacy Outdoors category contradicted the listing's tags and description, so the audited taxonomy corrects it to Indoors.

## What was not re-audited

This was a taxonomy review, not a full venue-facts refresh. Prices, hours, programming calendars, and individual dog policies beyond the agreed Outdoors/Water default remain outside this pass.
